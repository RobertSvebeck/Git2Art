Code is a shared art

Behind every public repository are people who chose to share their work: the owner, every contributor, the authors of the libraries it builds on, and these days often an AI working alongside them. So who is the artist? We think it is all of them. Good software has always been made together, and Git2Art paints that collaboration.

Every work in the museum carries the name of the people behind it, links to their profile, and leads to a room of their own. If a picture moves you, go and star the code. In open source, a star is the applause.
